## Deliverable

Python fitting against the differentiable simulator: bounded parameter transforms, gradient/finite-difference checks, multi-start fit, `L_params` accounting and result manifest.

## Done when

Controlled synthetic parameters recover within known statistical uncertainty and optimization budget is logged.

## Result

Implemented in [`python/occamworm/fit/`](../../../python/occamworm/fit/README.md) on the JAX simulator of OW-009.

* **Bounded transforms (§7.5).** `theta = lower + (upper - lower) sigmoid(raw)` from the IR bounds of every trainable parameter, with `lower + softplus(raw)` for one-sided bounds; exact inverses, checked monotone and inside the bounds.
* **Gradient checks.** `FitProblem.check_gradient` compares the `jax.value_and_grad` gradient in transformed coordinates with central finite differences: maximum relative error 2e-10 (adaptation circuit, Gaussian and AR(1) losses) and 4e-9 (gap circuit); the JAX AR(1) NLL equals `analysis.scoring.ar1_nll` to 1e-12 and its gradient matches finite differences.
* **Multi-start.** scipy L-BFGS-B on `raw`, start 0 at the declared values and the others a seeded Latin hypercube; identical seed gives identical starts and results. Budget counted per start and in total: function-and-gradient evaluations, iterations, simulated trial-steps, wall time (optional wall cap and per-start `maxiter`/`maxfun`, tested).
* **`L_params`.** Declared-precision bits of the fitted parameters (equal to the IR's `l_params.total_bits` when all are fitted: 12 + 10 + 10 = 32 bits for the test rule), `L_struct` from the IR, `L_total` their sum; estimates are also reported on the declared `2^bits` grid.
* **Manifest.** Strict JSON with program hash, grammar version, compiler build, estimates, loss per start, iterations, requested and used budget, seed and `L_struct`/`L_params`/`L_total`.
* **Loss interface.** Masked Gaussian NLL (known noise SD, or profiled) and the masked AR(1) NLL on `(trials, samples, neurons)` traces with a boolean mask.

**Recovery results** (`tests/synthetic_truth/test_parameter_recovery.py`, 8-neuron circuits with E/I chemical edges with delays and gap junctions, 6 stimulus trials of 100 steps, 10% of samples masked, 5 starts, fixed seeds). SE is the inverse-Hessian standard error; the parametric bootstrap (16 replicates) agrees within a factor of two in every case.

| Rule and noise | Parameter | True | Estimate | SE (Hessian) | SE (bootstrap) | z |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| adaptation circuit, Gaussian SD 0.05 | `tau_v` | 0.350 | 0.3490 | 0.0006 | 0.0008 | -1.50 |
| | `gain` | 1.600 | 1.5947 | 0.0040 | 0.0049 | -1.34 |
| | `adapt` | 0.900 | 0.8877 | 0.0068 | 0.0086 | -1.81 |
| gap + chemical circuit, AR(1) SD 0.05, phi 0.6 | `tau` | 0.400 | 0.3978 | 0.0023 | 0.0023 | -0.93 |
| | `g_scale` | 2.500 | 2.5625 | 0.0350 | 0.0353 | +1.79 |
| | `w` | 0.900 | 0.9002 | 0.0004 | 0.0004 | +0.52 |

All six estimates satisfy `|estimate - truth| < 3 SE`. Calibration over 16 replicate noise draws of the adaptation circuit (3 parameters each): 100% of z-scores inside 3, mean z squared 0.79 (expected 1), largest |z| 2.17. Profiled-noise Gaussian fits give the same estimates. Observed behaviour worth knowing: in the gap circuit some starts stop in poorer local minima (for example the declared start at NLL 77104 against -7751 for the others), so the multi-start and `loss_per_start` are doing real work; a fit from 5 starts took about 2 s (210 to 340 function-and-gradient evaluations) for 6 trials of 100 steps on 8 neurons.

**Deviations from GRAMMAR.md §5.7.** The spec lets `L_params` depend on fitted values; v0.1 of the bit code (WRL_SYNTAX.md §9) charges declared precision only, which is what is implemented. Not done: G0 fitting (no continuous parameters), hierarchical or per-neuron parameter sharing strategies of §7.5 (one global value per parameter only), posterior predictive checks for stochastic inference (G0/G1 are deterministic).
