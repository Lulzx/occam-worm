## Deliverable

Masked proper scoring with correlated-noise or block-scoring option, calcium filtering with a separately parameterized indicator stage, and synthetic calibration tests.

## Done when

Well-specified synthetic model is calibrated and predictions are scored consistently under missing observations.

## Progress

- [x] Masked proper scoring under correlated noise: an exact Gaussian AR(1) likelihood over the observed samples of each trace, with no imputation (AR(1) is Markov across gaps). Per-trace scale is σ² = a²s² + b², where s is the trace's own pre-stimulus SD and a, b, φ are fitted on training residuals with an analytic gradient. A block-aggregated alternative scores block means with their exact variance.
- [x] The indicator is parameterized separately from neural kernels: a difference of exponentials with optional saturation, estimated from training-fold autoresponses with an animal bootstrap, plus the resolvable-bandwidth calculation of §4.5 step 2.
- [x] Animal-level bootstrap of paired score differences (§11.5).
- [x] Done-when tests:
  - The masked NLL equals the multivariate-normal marginal of the observed samples to 1e-10 relative.
  - With 0 % and 40 % missing samples, the fitted noise recovers a, b and φ, and 50/80/95 % intervals cover within ±2 %.
  - The well-specified mean beats a misspecified one under missing data.
  - Indicator time constants are recovered.

Deviation: implemented in Python rather than `libs/ow-observe/` and `libs/ow-likelihood/` (§14.1, language follows the bottleneck); fitting already lives in Python. Real-data finding (benchmark folds): Gaussian AR(1) residuals over-cover the 50 % interval (≈0.65), while 95 % coverage is close to nominal (≈0.98). The residuals are heavier-tailed than Gaussian; a Student-t variant stays open under decision 3.
