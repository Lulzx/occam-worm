## Progress

- [x] Trial windows: 10 volumes before and 60 from each stimulation, dF/F against the trial's own pre-stimulus baseline, masked at tracking failures and at the next stimulation. Cached under `data/derived/`, keyed by the import manifest.
- [x] `python -m occamworm.analysis audit [--publish]` computes every §2.3 item from the import and the frozen thresholds in `configs/datasets/eligibility.json`, and writes `audit.json`, three Parquet tables and `report.md`. Re-running at the same revision reproduces `audit.json` byte for byte.
- [x] Result: **go**. 159 of 258 identified targets are eligible; 144 are covered by the chosen leave-one-animal-out split. Generated report: [AUDIT_REPORT.md](../../data/AUDIT_REPORT.md).
- [x] Neuron-ID intersection read from the [OW-013](OW-013.md) outputs: 206 atlas IDs resolve exactly or by an unambiguous alias, and all 206 are in the anatomy; 194 also have a transmitter assignment. 70 atlas labels are ambiguous (mostly left/right unspecified) and 28 are unresolved.

Findings that bind later tickets: every animal has one recording, so animal and session splits coincide; stimulations are about 31 s apart, so carryover is plausible; autoresponses shrink slightly with stimulation index and baselines bleach by about 2 %/min, so history variants (§4.1) are required.
