## Deliverable

The nested rule search of [PREREGISTRATION_M4.md](../../evaluation/PREREGISTRATION_M4.md) on all 113 LOAO folds, with its controls, sensitivity rows and locked analysis.

## Done when

The frozen pre-registration has been executed without unlogged deviations, and every criterion, control and sensitivity row is reported from a frozen run.

## Before launch

- [ ] Enumerator: the `gap_variants` and `gap_scale` keys (`configs/searches/g1-m4.json`), with C++ tests. `sum_in` `all` is already supported.
- [ ] Search:
  - [ ] Pilot stage (inner split 0, 1 start) and top-K full evaluation.
  - [ ] Fold-level worker processes.
  - [ ] The observation family (`student_t_ar1`) passed to every candidate's noise fit.
  - [ ] Per-view caching of designs and pair statistics, which are the same for every T2a candidate but recomputed today. Reuse compiled or traced programs where possible. The pilot costs 3.5 s per program, most of it overhead.
- [ ] Baseline track inside the search: all ten families (B1d, B2-K*, B4-fixed and B4-learned are not search candidates yet).
- [ ] T2b in the search uses the experiment's indicator (`configured_indicator`). `candidates._indicator` still fits autoresponses.
- [ ] Controls: degree-preserving randomized chemical (signed) and gap graphs; stimulus-target label shuffle within recordings; the full re-search on the pre-declared 10 folds.
- [ ] Locked analysis script for §7–§10, including:
  - non-responsive pairs taken from outer-training animals only;
  - day-clustered bootstrap;
  - gap fraction.
- [ ] Measured volume and cost projection recorded here; volume rule of §4 applied.
- [ ] Pre-registration frozen: registry entry with the three sha256s, and the status line changed.
