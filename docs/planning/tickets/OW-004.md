## Deliverable

Immutable outer/inner splits with validation of animal/session exclusivity.

## Done when

Deliberately leaked animal/session fixture raises a hard error, splits are seed-stable, and the chosen scheme is justified by the OW-003 coverage tables (including a leave-one-animal-out comparison) and recorded before any model is scored.

## Progress

- [x] Folds are dealt by `sha256(seed:scope:animal)` order, independent of numpy and of input order. Inner folds are grouped by animal inside each outer training set.
- [x] Leakage checks raise `LeakageError` for an animal in both train and test, an animal tested twice, an outer-test animal in the inner loop, a session shared across animals or folds, unknown animals, and duplicate ids ([tests/leakage/](../../../tests/leakage/)).
- [x] Frozen split files carry a content hash. Editing one, or rewriting it with different content, raises.
- [x] Scheme chosen from the OW-003 coverage tables alone, before any model was scored: leave-one-animal-out (113 outer folds, 5 inner grouped folds, seed 20261008) covers 144 eligible targets, against 125 for 5-fold and 127 for 10-fold. Frozen as [`data/splits/randi2023-wt-v1-loao/split.json`](../../../data/splits/randi2023-wt-v1-loao/split.json) and recorded in the [experiment registry](../../evaluation/EXPERIMENT_REGISTRY.md#registered-entries).
