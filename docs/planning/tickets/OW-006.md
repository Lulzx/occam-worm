## Deliverable

B0–B3 reference models with matching output API and regularization selection, each in history and no-history variants, evaluated on T2a and T2b.

## Done when

Known synthetic shared- and independent-kernel datasets favor the correctly specified family under adequate power, and the synthetic false-sharing control ([§4.5](../../science/BASELINES.md)) reports its rate at the measured noise level.

## Progress

- [x] B0 (null drift), B1 (one kernel per target), B1d (B1 with per-pair delays of −2 to +2 volumes), B2-K{1,2,4,8} (a global bank of K kernels) and B3 (independent kernels). All of them use one tent basis and one output API. Ridge λ is selected on inner folds, with warm starts across the grid. There are history and no-history variants, on T2a (measured input) and T2b (nominal impulse through a fitted indicator).
- [x] Synthetic truth (`tests/synthetic_truth/test_kernel_families.py`): data generated with a shared kernel favour B1 and data generated with independent kernels favour B3, both on held-out animals.
- [x] The false-sharing control (`python -m occamworm.baselines control`, §4.5) writes `indicator_control.json`.
- [ ] Full LOAO benchmark (`artifacts/baselines-v1`, 113 outer folds × 4 task/history configurations). Then `summarize`, `control`, `freeze` and `report`.

Development result (outer folds 0–1, T2a): B3 ≳ B2-K8 > B1d ≈ B1 > B0, and the history term helps slightly.

Fix (2026-10-08, 8d7f60b): inner selection used to score every ridge value with one noise model, fitted at the middle grid value. That pushed all kernel families to the top grid edge and B4 to the bottom edge on every completed fold. On fold 0, B1 at λ=100 scores −658006 inner NLL with its own noise, against −637172 with the shared noise, and beats both B0 (−651194) and B4. Each ridge value now gets noise fitted on its own training residuals, and a regression test checks that a value's inner score does not depend on the rest of the grid. The grid is now 0.01 to 1000, and the summary reports how often each family selects a grid edge. The first partial run (15 folds) was discarded and restarted.

Run settings (f0de9f4): two speed-ups.

- Inner-selection noise models are fitted on a deterministic subsample of 10k training traces, about 500k samples for 3 parameters. Outer refits keep 30k.
- B4 compiles its objective once per model.

Measured: about 1,340 s per fold, against 3,550 s before (2.6×). With 11 workers instead of 9, the run is about 3.2× faster overall: roughly 3.8 h per task/history configuration.

Interim result (T2a without history, all 113 outer folds; 110 held-out animals, since 3 have no eligible traces; code f0de9f4). The figures are paired held-out ΔNLL per animal with 95% animal-bootstrap CIs; positive means the second model is better.

- B0 → B3: +105 (+22 to +182); 66% of animals favour B3.
- B0 → B1: +102 (+24 to +176).
- B3 → B1: −3 (−7 to +1). One shape per target is not distinguishable from free per-pair kernels.
- B3 → B2-K4: +0.01 (−0.17 to +0.21). Four shared shapes are equivalent to free per-pair kernels.
- Nested selection picks B2-K4 in 85 of 110 folds. Its held-out total is 652 NLL lower than B3's.
- No kernel family selects a ridge value at a grid edge. Central 50% and 95% intervals cover 62% and 96% of held-out samples, which matches the heavier-than-Gaussian tails seen earlier.

Run status: the benchmark runs from a pinned worktree at f0de9f4, so that other sessions' working-tree edits cannot reach its workers. It pauses when the Mac sleeps or runs low on battery, and resumes fold by fold. T2a-hist is at 36 of 113 folds; T2b has not started.

T2b runs as a separate run (2026-10-12). It needs the indicator-fit fix in dd15448: slow, near-alpha mean responses drove the old (τr, τd − τr) fit to τd = τr, and the fit crashed. Only T2b fits the indicator, so the run splits in two:

- `artifacts/baselines-v1`: T2a with and without history, code f0de9f4.
- `artifacts/baselines-v2-t2b`: T2b with and without history, plus the §4.5 control, from a second pinned worktree, `../occam-worm-bench-t2b`. It uses experiment `shared-timescales-wt-v2`: the indicator is fixed at published GCaMP6s kinetics (τr = 0.4 s, τd = 2.5 s; [decision 14](../OPEN_DECISIONS.md)), not fitted to autoresponses. A fold-0 smoke test of the fitted version gave τr ≈ τd ≈ 11.5 s, the alpha limit. That is the stimulated neuron's whole calcium response, not the sensor. When this changed, the only T2b outer fold scored was that smoke test, and its held-out scores were not looked at.

The §4.5 resolvable bandwidth was also redefined before T2b started. The old version compared a typical response amplitude with the noise of a single sample, so it gave 0 Hz whatever the indicator. A kernel component at frequency f now counts as resolvable when its matched-filter SNR is at least 1. That SNR integrates over the kernel window (30 s) and the median trial count of a responsive pair (2), after the indicator's attenuation. With T2a noise and the median responsive amplitude, the published indicator resolves up to about 0.085 Hz (a 1.9 s timescale). The slow corner of its range resolves up to 0.024 Hz (6.6 s), and the autoresponse fit up to 0.011 Hz (15 s). The control reports both the central value and the slow corner.

Each run is summarized, frozen and reported on its own. The driver runs as a LaunchAgent, so it survives reboots. It pauses only when the battery is below 25%, and resumes fold by fold. An earlier policy paused on every unplug; that threw away the folds in progress (about 20 minutes each), and a reboot on 2026-10-12 killed the nohup-launched driver.
