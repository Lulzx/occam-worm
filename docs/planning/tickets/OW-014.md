## Deliverable

Top-diverse candidates, approximate predictive posterior, candidate intervention scorer.

## Done when

Planner selects known discriminative stimuli in synthetic model pairs and reports information-gain uncertainty.

## Progress

- [x] Program posterior: weights proportional to the cross-validated likelihood times `2^-L_struct` (§7.7), effective sample size, top-diverse selection by predictive distance; §8.2 hypothesis records.
- [x] Planner: expected information gain by predictive Monte Carlo under the fitted AR(1) noise, with a Monte Carlo standard error per intervention; utility = EIG − cost weight × cost (§8.3).
- [x] Equivalence: exact divergence under shared AR(1) noise, empirical maximum over an observer's tested interventions, maximal cliques of the indistinguishability graph, and operational necessity of ablations (§9).
- [x] Done-when test: with two synthetic hypotheses the planner ranks first the stimulus that separates them (EIG ≈ ln 2, the maximum for two equally weighted hypotheses); the stimulus both predict identically gets zero gain; a divergence buried in noise gets a small gain with a reported standard error.

Deviation: implemented in Python instead of the proposed `libs/ow-infer/`, `libs/ow-experiments/` and `libs/ow-equivalence/`, following §14.1 ("language follows the bottleneck"); none of it is compute-bound. Running it on real candidates waits for the frozen search output of [OW-012](OW-012.md).
